# Medicare
Medicare – Smart Healthcare Management Platform

Medicare is a comprehensive healthcare management web platform designed to digitally connect patients and doctors in a secure, efficient, and intelligent way. The system centralizes patient records, doctor (admin) controls, prescriptions, and medication management into a single, easy-to-use interface.

In Medicare, doctors act as administrators, allowing them to securely access patient information, interact with patients in real time, and prescribe medications directly through the platform. Patients can view their prescriptions instantly, select medications, and maintain a clear digital medical history without paperwork.

A key highlight of Medicare is its AI-powered medical assistance. Patients can describe symptoms such as headaches or common health issues, and the AI provides doctor-approved guidance, ensuring reliability while reducing unnecessary delays. This feature is designed to assist—not replace—professional medical consultation.

The project was built by students of Assam Engineering College as part of CodeWare 7.0, a 24-hour hackathon conducted during the Pyrokinesis event. Medicare represents our vision of combining technology, AI, and healthcare to make medical access more streamlined and patient-friendly.
